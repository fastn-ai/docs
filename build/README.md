# Build

Build is where integrations are made. Nine pages, in roughly the order you meet them.

Six of them match a screen under **Integrations** in the sidebar. **Build with AI** has its own sidebar item and is also reached from the Home *What should your customers be able to do?* prompt, and *MCP gateway* and *Common patterns* have no screen of their own. They cut across several. The **Widget** lives under Embed and is documented under [Embed](../embed/README.md).
