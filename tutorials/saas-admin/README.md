# Tutorials for your team

These tutorials are for the person responsible for fastn inside your company, often someone in operations or a product manager who would rather not go deep into the technical details. You'll configure the platform, preview and publish the integration experience, and manage your customers.

**Prerequisites:** Complete the Getting Started section first. These tutorials assume you have a fastn account and have completed onboarding (Connect your product, Choose integrations, Embed the widget, Customers connect and customize).

### Tutorials in this section

[**Setting Up Your Organization**](setting-up-your-organization.md) - Configure organization settings, invite your team via Settings → People, review plan limits and the audit log, and find API keys, secrets and environments.

[**Creating a Workflow via AI**](creating-a-workflow-via-ai.md) - Use the "Build with AI" button to create workflows by describing what you need in plain language.

[**Building Your Widget**](building-your-widget.md) - An overview of how to customize, preview and publish your widget for your customers.

[**Managing Customers**](managing-customers.md): create customers under Operate → Customers, scope API calls per customer, and monitor customer-level activity.

[**Roles & Permissions Setup** ](roles-and-permissions-setup.md) - Assign system roles (Owner, Admin, Developer, Operator, Viewer, End User), create custom roles, and manage team access.
