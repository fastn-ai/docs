# Dashboard tutorials

These tutorials are for the person on your team responsible for fastn, often someone in operations or a product manager who would rather not go deep into the technical details. You configure fastn, preview and publish the widget, and manage your customers.

**Prerequisites:** Complete [Set up fastn](../../getting-started/onboarding/README.md) first. These tutorials assume you have a fastn account and have completed onboarding setup (Check your product, Pick your integrations, Set up the widget).

### Tutorials in this section

[**Set up your organization**](setting-up-your-organization.md) - Configure organization settings, invite your team via Settings → People, review plan limits and the audit log, and find API keys, secrets, and environments.

[**Create a workflow with the fastn agent**](creating-a-workflow-via-ai.md) - Use **Build with fastn agent** to create workflows by describing what you need in plain language. Needs a paid plan (Scale or Enterprise).

[**Build your widget**](building-your-widget.md) - An overview of how to customize, preview, and publish your widget for your customers.

[**Manage customers**](managing-customers.md) - Create customers under Operate → Customers, scope API calls per customer, and monitor customer-level activity.

[**Set up roles and permissions**](roles-and-permissions-setup.md) - Assign built-in roles (Owner, Admin, Developer, Operator, Viewer, End User), create custom roles, and manage team access.
