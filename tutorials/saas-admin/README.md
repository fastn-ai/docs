# Dashboard tutorials

These tutorials are for the person on your team responsible for fastn, often someone in operations or a product manager who would rather not go deep into the technical details. You'll configure the platform, preview and publish the integration experience, and manage your customers.

**Prerequisites:** Complete the Getting Started section first. These tutorials assume you have a fastn account and have completed onboarding setup (Check your product, Pick your integrations, Set up the widget).

### Tutorials in this section

[**Setting Up Your Organization**](setting-up-your-organization.md) - Configure organization settings, invite your team via Settings → People, review plan limits and the audit log, and find API keys, secrets and environments.

[**Creating a Workflow via AI**](creating-a-workflow-via-ai.md) - Use **Build with fastn agent** to create workflows by describing what you need in plain language. Needs a paid plan (Scale or Enterprise).

[**Building Your Widget**](building-your-widget.md) - An overview of how to customize, preview and publish your widget for your customers.

[**Managing Customers**](managing-customers.md) - Create customers under Operate → Customers, scope API calls per customer, and monitor customer-level activity.

[**Roles & Permissions Setup**](roles-and-permissions-setup.md) - Assign built-in roles (Owner, Admin, Developer, Operator, Viewer, End User), create custom roles, and manage team access.
