# For your customers

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lifecycle/fastn-lifecycle-4-customize.svg" alt="Stage 4, Customize: the end-customer admin connects accounts and adapts integrations in the widget"><figcaption><p>Where this fits: stage 4 of the integration lifecycle</p></figcaption></figure>

These tutorials are for the people at your customer who use integrations through the widget embedded in a SaaS product. You don't need a fastn account or dashboard access - everything happens inside the product you already use.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note for SaaS companies:** Share this section with your customers when they ask how to use the integrations you've embedded. These tutorials describe the customer experience in the widget, not the fastn dashboard.
{% endhint %}

### Tutorials in this section

[**Connecting Apps via Widget**](connecting-apps-via-widget.md) - Find an integration, sign in to your account on the app, and manage or remove what you set up.

[**Customizing Your Integrations**](customizing-your-integrations.md) - Adjust field mappings, filters and other settings for an integration you've set up.

[**Extending a Pre-Built Integration**](extending-a-prebuilt-integration.md) - Change what fields, filters and direction a shipped integration uses, and know where the assistant's authority ends.

[**Getting Alerted**](getting-alerted.md) - Set up your own alerts so a broken or stalled integration tells you, instead of a colleague telling you.

[**Connect Claude and Other AI Clients**](connecting-claude.md) - Generate a one-time MCP URL from the widget's **⋯** menu and drive your integrations from Claude, with no fastn account.

[**Viewing Sync Status & History**](viewing-sync-status-and-history.md) - Check what's syncing, view run history, and troubleshoot when something fails.
