# For your customers

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/lifecycle/fastn-lifecycle-4-customize.svg" alt="Stage 4, Customize: the customer's admin connects accounts and adapts integrations in the widget"><figcaption><p>Where this fits: stage 4 of the integration lifecycle</p></figcaption></figure>

These tutorials are for the people at your customer who use integrations through the widget embedded in your product. They don't need a fastn account or dashboard access: everything happens inside your product.

{% hint style="info" %}
**For you, the product team:** Share this section with your customers when they ask how to use the integrations you've embedded, or copy it into your own help center. The pages speak to your customer as "you", call your product "the product you use", and describe the widget, not the fastn dashboard.
{% endhint %}

### Tutorials in this section

[**Connect apps**](connecting-apps-via-widget.md): find an integration, sign in to your account on the app, and manage or remove what you set up.

[**Customize an integration**](customizing-your-integrations.md): adjust field mappings, filters and other settings for an integration you've set up.

[**Extend a pre-built integration**](extending-a-prebuilt-integration.md): change what fields, filters and direction a shipped integration uses, and know where the assistant's authority ends.

[**Get alerted**](getting-alerted.md): set up your own alerts so a broken or stalled integration tells you, instead of a colleague telling you.

[**Connect Claude and other AI clients**](connecting-claude.md): generate a one-time MCP URL from the widget's **⋯** menu and drive your integrations from Claude, with no fastn account.

[**View sync status and history**](viewing-sync-status-and-history.md): check what's syncing, view run history, and troubleshoot when something fails.
