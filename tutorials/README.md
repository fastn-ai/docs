# Tutorials

These tutorials are hands-on walkthroughs that teach you how to use fastn by building real things. Each tutorial has a clear outcome and by the end, you'll have something working.

**Prerequisites:** Read [Core concepts](../getting-started/concepts.md) first. These tutorials assume you have a fastn account and basic familiarity with the dashboard.

### Tutorials for your team

For the operator: the person on your team who sets up and runs fastn for your product.

[**Setting Up Your Organization**](saas-admin/setting-up-your-organization.md) - Configure organization settings, invite your team, review plan limits and the audit log, and find API keys, secrets, environments and connector auth.

[**Creating a Workflow via AI**](saas-admin/creating-a-workflow-via-ai.md) - Use the fastn agent to create workflows by describing what you need in plain language. Needs a paid plan (Scale or Enterprise).

[**Building Your Widget**](saas-admin/building-your-widget.md) - See how the widget builder works and what your customers see.

[**Managing Customers**](saas-admin/managing-customers.md) - Create customers, scope API calls, customize quota limits, and monitor activity.

[**Roles & Permissions Setup**](saas-admin/roles-and-permissions-setup.md) - Assign built-in roles (Owner, Admin, Developer, Operator, Viewer, End User), create custom roles, and manage access.

### Tutorials for the people at your customers

For the people at your customers who use integrations through the embedded widget in your product.

[**Connecting Apps via Widget**](end-user/connecting-apps-via-widget.md) - Find available integrations, authorize your accounts, and activate connections.

[**Customizing Your Integrations**](end-user/customizing-your-integrations.md) - Configure sync preferences, field mappings, and scheduling.

[**Viewing Sync Status & History**](end-user/viewing-sync-status-and-history.md) - Check what's syncing, view history, and troubleshoot failures.
