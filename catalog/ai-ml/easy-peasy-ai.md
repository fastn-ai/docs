## Setup

Your customers sign in to Easy-Peasy.AI through Fastn's own OAuth app, so nothing needs setting up first. To show your own app on the consent screen instead, add your OAuth app on the connector's **Auth** tab under **Manage providers**.

## Use it

Add Easy-Peasy.AI from **Build → Integrations → Connectors** and connect an account, as in [Connect a system](../../build/connectors/connect-a-system.md). In workflow code, call an action as `fastn.connector.easyPeasyAi.<action>()`, with the action's slug from the table below. See [Workflow runtime API](../../reference/workflow-runtime.md).

## Events

Easy-Peasy.AI has no events, so it can't start a workflow by itself. Use its actions in a workflow that another [trigger](../../build/triggers/README.md) starts, such as a schedule or a webhook.
