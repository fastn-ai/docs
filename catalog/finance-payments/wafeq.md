## Setup

Your customers sign in to Wafeq through Fastn's own OAuth app, so nothing needs setting up first. To show your own app on the consent screen instead, add your OAuth app on the connector's **Auth** tab under **Manage providers**.

## Connect in Fastn

Your customers connect Wafeq from your widget. To connect an account yourself:

1. Go to **Build → Integrations → Connectors** and search for **Wafeq**.
2. Select **Connect** on the Wafeq card.

   <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/connector-guides/fastn/connect-search.png" alt="The Connectors page in Fastn with ServiceNow typed in the search box and the ServiceNow card showing a Connect button"><figcaption><p>Search for the connector, then select Connect on its card. ServiceNow is shown here.</p></figcaption></figure>

3. Wafeq offers more than one sign-in method. Choose one in **Auth Method**, then:

   * For a key or credential method, fill in the fields the form asks for, using the values from [Setup](#setup) above. Then select **Connect**.

     <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/connector-guides/fastn/connect-credentials.png" alt="The Connect ServiceNow dialog with an optional account name and the Instance, Username and Password fields, and Cancel and Connect buttons"><figcaption><p>A connect form that asks for credentials. Each connector asks for its own fields.</p></figcaption></figure>

   * For OAuth 2.0, keep the **Default** auth provider, or choose your own OAuth app if you added one. Pick the **Scopes** you need, select **Authorize**, then sign in to Wafeq and approve access in the window that opens.

     <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/connector-guides/fastn/connect-oauth.png" alt="The Connect Salesforce dialog for OAuth 2.0 with the Default auth provider, an optional account name, the Scopes selector and an Authorize button"><figcaption><p>A connect form for OAuth 2.0. Salesforce is shown here.</p></figcaption></figure>

4. Check that the connection appears under **Operate → Connections** with the status `Active`. Your workflows can now use Wafeq's actions.

For more detail, see [Connect an app](../../build/connectors/connect-an-app.md).
