## Setup

Your customers sign in to Microsoft OneDrive through Fastn's own OAuth app, so nothing needs setting up first. To show your own app on the consent screen instead, add your OAuth app on the connector's **Auth** tab under **Manage providers**.

## Connect in Fastn

Your customers connect Microsoft OneDrive from your widget. To connect an account yourself:

1. Go to **Build → Integrations → Connectors** and search for **Microsoft OneDrive**.
2. Select **Connect** on the Microsoft OneDrive card.

   <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/connector-guides/fastn/connect-search.png" alt="The Connectors page in Fastn with ServiceNow typed in the search box and the ServiceNow card showing a Connect button"><figcaption><p>Search for the connector, then select Connect on its card. ServiceNow is shown here.</p></figcaption></figure>

3. Keep the **Default** auth provider, or choose your own OAuth app if you added one. Pick the **Scopes** you need, select **Authorize**, then sign in to Microsoft OneDrive and approve access in the window that opens.

   <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/connector-guides/fastn/connect-oauth.png" alt="The Connect Salesforce dialog for OAuth 2.0 with the Default auth provider, an optional account name, the Scopes selector and an Authorize button"><figcaption><p>A connect form for OAuth 2.0. Salesforce is shown here.</p></figcaption></figure>

4. Check that the connection appears under **Operate → Connections** with the status `Active`. Your workflows can now use Microsoft OneDrive's actions.

For more detail, see [Connect an app](../../build/connectors/connect-an-app.md).
