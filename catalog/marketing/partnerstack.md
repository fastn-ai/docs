## Setup

Your customers enter their access token for PartnerStack (Partner key) when they connect. Nothing else needs setting up.

## Use it

Add PartnerStack (Partner key) from **Build → Integrations → Connectors** and connect an account, as in [Connect a system](../../build/connectors/connect-a-system.md). In workflow code, call an action as `fastn.connector.partnerstack.<action>()`, with the action's slug from the table below. See [Workflow runtime API](../../reference/workflow-runtime.md).

## Actions

<table><thead><tr><th width="240">Action</th><th width="260">Slug</th><th>What it does</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Get Marketplace Program</td><td><code>getMarketplaceProgram</code></td><td>Retrieves a single PartnerStack marketplace program by its company key, including its categories, description, commission offers and promotions. Use the company key from a partnership returned by List Partnerships. Note that keys returned by List Marketplace Programs are not always resolvable here and can return a 404.</td></tr><tr><td>List Actions</td><td><code>listPartnerActions</code></td><td>Returns the actions you or your referred customers performed on a partner company's platform, such as a signup or a content submission, sorted by creation date with the most recent first. Each action carries its type, how many times it was performed, and the partnership and customer it belongs to. Results are cursor paginated.</td></tr><tr><td>List Customers</td><td><code>listCustomers</code></td><td>Returns the customers you referred to the companies you are partnered with, sorted by creation date with the most recent first. Each customer carries the referring partnership, the company, whether the customer has paid and any sub IDs used for tracking. Results are cursor paginated.</td></tr><tr><td>List Marketplace Programs</td><td><code>listMarketplacePrograms</code></td><td>Returns the partner programs listed on the PartnerStack marketplace that you can apply to join, sorted by creation date with the most recent first. Each program carries its categories, description, website, commission offers and available promotions. Results are cursor paginated.</td></tr><tr><td>List Partnerships</td><td><code>listPartnerships</code></td><td>Returns the partnerships between you and the companies whose programs you promote on PartnerStack, sorted by creation date with the most recent first. Each partnership carries the company, your status in its program, your referral link and your partner team. Results are cursor paginated.</td></tr><tr><td>List Payouts</td><td><code>listPayouts</code></td><td>Returns your payouts, the rewards that have been made payable to you, sorted by creation date with the most recent first. Each payout carries its amount in cents, currency, status, payment provider and withdrawal date. Results are cursor paginated.</td></tr><tr><td>List Rewards</td><td><code>listRewards</code></td><td>Returns the rewards you have earned from the companies you are partnered with, sorted by creation date with the most recent first. Each reward carries its amount in cents, reward and payment status, the source that generated it and the customer it relates to. Supports extensive filtering by company, group, customer, invoice, currency, status and free text keywords. Results are cursor paginated.</td></tr><tr><td>List Transactions</td><td><code>listTransactions</code></td><td>Returns the transactions recorded against the customers you referred, sorted by creation date with the most recent first. Each transaction carries its amount in cents, the currency, the originating customer and partnership, and the product it relates to. Results are cursor paginated.</td></tr></tbody></table>
