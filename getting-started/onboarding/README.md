# Onboarding

Onboarding, at `/onboarding`, takes you from signing up to a customer connecting an app inside your product. It has two parts: **Setup**, three steps you do in fastn, and **Going live**, four items that happen in your product, two of them optional. Your progress shows on Home in the **Get started** card, as a count out of 7.

### Setup

| Step | What you do | Done when | AI credits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Check your product** | fastn checks your domain and researches your product, then shows **Here's what we found**. Click **Looks right** or **Edit** on each row. | You confirm the description, and the research has run | 15 |
| **2. Pick your integrations** | Pick every app your customers need. Each app that has a connector in fastn appears in your widget, ready for customers to connect, with nothing to build. See [Pick your integrations](choose-integrations.md). | Your widget offers at least one app, or the whole catalog is on | 10 |
| **3. Set up the widget** | Make the widget match your product, and choose who adds it: **I'll add it**, **My developer** or **My AI tool**. See [Embed the widget](embed-the-widget.md). | The widget loads in your product, or a customer connects through it | 5 |

### Going live

| Item | What happens | Done when | AI credits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Add the widget to your product** | Your developer follows four parts: create an API key, register each customer, get an embed token, add the iframe. See [Embed the widget](embed-the-widget.md#developer-setup). | The widget loads on a page of your own | 0 |
| **Your first customer connects** | You open your product as a test customer and connect an app in the widget. See [Customers connect and customize](customers-connect-and-customize.md). | A customer connects an app | 0 |
| **Connect your product** (optional) | An admin registers your product's API as a connector. Needed when an integration reads or writes your product's data. See [Connect your product](connect-your-product.md). | Your product is registered | 0 |
| **Build an integration** (optional) | Build a workflow that syncs or automates for the apps you picked: in your AI tool on every plan, or with the fastn agent on Scale and Enterprise. See [Pick your integrations](choose-integrations.md#build-an-integration). | One of your widgets runs a workflow | 20 |

### AI credits

Onboarding can earn up to 50 AI credits: 15, 10 and 5 for the setup steps, and 20 for building an integration. They are used after any monthly credits and never expire. Launch has no monthly AI credits from 1 December 2026, and Launch accounts created from 25 November 2026 start with none, so these are a Launch account's only AI credits. Building in your own AI tool uses none. See [Billing and limits](../../manage/billing.md#credits).

To build a first integration outside onboarding, see [Quickstart: your first integration](../quickstart.md).

**Get help:** ask fastn's agent questions (the prompt on **Home**, or `/agent`, on every plan), or contact the fastn team from the help menu in fastn.
