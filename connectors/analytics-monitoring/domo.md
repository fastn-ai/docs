## Setup

Your customers sign in to Domo through fastn's own OAuth app, so nothing needs setting up first. To show your own app on the consent screen instead, add your OAuth app on the connector's **Auth** tab under **Manage providers**.

## Use it

Add Domo from **Build → Integrations → Connectors** and connect an account, as in [Connect a system](../../build/connectors/connect-a-system.md). In workflow code, call an action as `fastn.connector.domo.<action>()`, with the action's slug from the table below. See [Workflow runtime API](../../reference/workflow-runtime.md).

## Events

Domo has no events, so it can't start a workflow by itself. Use its actions in a workflow that another [trigger](../../build/triggers/README.md) starts, such as a schedule or a webhook.
